The diff beside the agent, in every workspace

Click the terminal (or tab into it). d opens the diff at the right of the panels, not over them; d again closes it. Its keys are the diff's own (j/k change file, 1 2 3 the view: uncommitted, commits, general); pick lines with V, and ↵ writes where they are in the agent's field so you can ask about them. The buttons change what the screen shows.
