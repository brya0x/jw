Closing things, and the theme

The terminal client gets the keys to close a pane, close a workspace and remove a worktree, each asking only when something would be lost, and a settings box (,) to choose the theme. Click the terminal or tab into it; the keys are on its status bar. The theme you pick here repaints the whole page, and the same ten palettes (five dark, five light) and three modes are on the phone.

To approve

    
    
      
  1. Close a pane: x on the focused pane (↵ or h/l focuses one). A shell sitting at its prompt closes at once. If a program runs in it (claude, nvim, a command still going) a box asks first: y cerrar · esc cancelar, and says what stops. A workspace may be left with no panes.
    2. 
      
  2. Close a workspace: X. One box: the panes end (it names any program still running) and the folder or worktree stays on disk. For a worktree the box also offers d, remove the worktree.
    3. 
      
  3. Remove a worktree: d from that box. It never forces. The client looks at the uncommitted files first: with none, y quitar deletes the folder (the branch stays); with some, the box lists them and says they must be committed or dropped first, and offers c to close the workspace and leave everything. A branch with commits not pushed is safe: the branch is kept. Use the first button to see both cases.
    4. 
      
  4. Theme: , opens settings. Modo is sistema, oscuro or claro. The list under it shows only the themes of that kind: with oscuro, the dark ones (One Dark, Catppuccin Mocha, Tokyo Night, Gruvbox Dark, Solarized Dark); with claro, the light ones (One Light, Catppuccin Latte, Tokyo Day, Gruvbox Light, Solarized Light). Each kind remembers its own choice, so changing the mode brings back the theme you last picked for it. Sistema follows the machine and shows the list of what the machine is in now. Choosing applies at once and is kept in settings.json. Try j/k, ↵ and m.
    5. 
      
  5. Not here: leader and rebindable keys, animations and your own themes (only the theme is in scope), and closing a workspace or removing a worktree from the phone (the phone only closes a pane).
