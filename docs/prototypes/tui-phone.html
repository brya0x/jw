Pairing the phone, from inside jwn

Today pairing is a separate command (jwn pair) in another terminal. Here it is a box of the terminal client: press m (after ^␣ when a pane has the keyboard, like every command of the client). Click the terminal or tab into it; the keys are on its status bar. The buttons simulate what the PC and the phone do.

  1. Open: m. It turns the phone link on (the same as jwn pair) and shows a box: the QR, the link, the local address and the tunnel when there is one. While it opens (a tunnel can take up to a minute) the box says so and the client keeps working. esc closes the box and leaves the link on; m shows it again; s turns the link off; c copies the link.

  2. The pairing question comes to you. When a phone scans and asks to pair, the box opens by itself, even if you had closed it: «¿Emparejar «Pixel 8»? El teléfono muestra el código 482 913», and only y accepts, any other key refuses. Then the box shows the link again, with how many phones are paired.

  3. No tunnel is not an error. Without cloudflared the box says «sin túnel: solo desde tu red» and the local address and the QR still work. A failure to turn the link on is a red box with the reason.
