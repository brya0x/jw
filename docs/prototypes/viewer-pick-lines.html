The file viewer with a cursor and tabs

Today the viewer only scrolls and shows one file. Here it has a cursor you move with h j k l, v opens a selection, and several files stay open in tabs. What is selected can be copied, or written as a reference in the field of the workspace's Claude (@src/auth.rs#L12-15), which sends nothing: you read it and press enter. Both screens work: click the terminal and press the keys.

Keys of the terminal viewer (what is new in blue)

    

      
h j k l  ← ↓ ↑ →new: the cursor moves one character or one line; the view follows it (two lines of margin). h l no longer only scroll sideways: with wrapping off the view goes sideways to keep the cursor in sight

      
vnew: opens a selection at the cursor; h j k l grow it by characters; v or esc let go

      
↵new: writes the reference in the field of Claude: @src/auth.rs#L12-15  for what is selected, #L12 for the line of the cursor when nothing is. Nothing is sent

      
ywith a selection it copies it; with none it copies the whole file, as today

      
enothing: no key opens an editor any more (N151)

      
Tab  ⇧Tabnew: next and previous tab; a click on a tab goes to it. Each tab keeps its own cursor, selection and place

      
q  escwith a selection esc lets go of it; otherwise esc or q close the tab, and the last one closes the viewer

      
↵ on a file of the treenew: with the viewer open it opens the file in a new tab (or goes to its tab if it is open); with it closed it opens the viewer, as today

      
j k space b g G / n N w r sunchanged where they do not collide: j k are now the cursor (the view follows it); space and b page, g G go to the top and the bottom (the cursor too), / n N search (the cursor goes to the match), w wraps, r rereads, s shows a Markdown's source

      
click · dragclick puts the cursor there; dragging selects, and on release it is copied, as a drag does in the conversation
