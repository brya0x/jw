^B: subagents, shells and monitors, each apart, and the keyboard

The area of the agents becomes the screen of everything Claude runs while you work, in three sections: subagentes, shells (what Claude runs in the background with Bash) and monitores (Monitor and the loops that watch or repeat). ^B opens it with the keys already in it; ↑↓ (or jk) walk every line across the sections, ←→ (or hl) jump a section, x stops the lit one. Click the terminal below and try; the clock runs.
