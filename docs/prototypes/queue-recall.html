↑ brings back what waits in the queue

While Claude works, what you send waits in the daemon's queue (⧖) and goes one message per turn. Today the only thing to do with a message that waits is to leave it. Here ↑ on the first row of the field takes the last one back into the field, out of the queue, to edit it, send it again or erase it. Click the terminal and type: Enter sends (it queues while Claude works), Ctrl+Enter sends now (N181a: interrupts and goes first), Esc interrupts. The choices at the bottom change what ↑ does here.

CONVERSATION


  

  
Keys: letters type, Enter sends (queues while a turn runs), Ctrl+Enter sends now, Shift+Enter a new line, ← → move, ↑ on the first row brings back the last message of the queue (the chip en cola N counts one less); with nothing left to bring back it goes through the sent messages as composer-history does (the chip ↑ i/n), ↓ on the last row goes forward in that history, Esc interrupts the turn that runs. To erase a message that waited: ↑ and then Ctrl+C (it clears the field when nothing runs; here also Ctrl+U) or Backspace.


  
WHAT CHANGES

    
    
      
  • ↑ on the first row (where it already recalls the history, REQ-714) looks first at the queue: if a message that you wrote waits, it comes out of the queue and into the field, the cursor at its start so another ↑ takes the one before. The messages that wait are the ones you wrote (REQ-382); they keep their order when they come back together.
    • 
      
  • The daemon does the taking, in one step: a command conversation_unqueue {node} whose answer is {text} (or nothing when no message of yours waits). It is one command because the daemon may take the first of the queue at the very moment the turn ends: if the client read the queue and then asked to remove the n-th, it could remove another. The last one is the farthest from being sent, so the race is rare, and when the queue has just emptied the answer is «nothing» and ↑ goes on to the history. The contract gains the command only (additive, no node's v changes; there is no PROTOCOL number in this repository, RAT-2).
    • 
      
  • What cannot come back stays. A /model, /effort or /mode that waits is the engine's own line, not a message, and what another claude queued (REQ-549) is not ours: both stay and ↑ skips them.
    • 
      
  • ↓, Esc and the history are as now. The history's draft takes whatever the field holds when ↑ goes on into the history, so nothing is lost there either.
