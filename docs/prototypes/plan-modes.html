Approving a plan goes back to the mode you had

Today the first answer of a plan always says «Aprobar y editar sin preguntar» and moves the engine to accept edits, even when you came into plan from auto. The daemon would remember the mode you were in before the plan, and the first answer would take you back to it when it was auto. Pick the mode you came from, then use the keys in the terminal (click it) or tap the phone.

  3. What the daemon does. It keeps the mode the engine reports just before it reports plan; it forgets it when the conversation leaves plan another way (⇧tab, /mode) and when it restarts (a new engine starts with the mode it is given, so none is remembered). The answer key becomes auto and the daemon asks the engine for auto at once, as it asks for acceptEdits today.
