Keep planning, with the next instruction

When Claude asks to approve a plan, «Seguir planeando» no longer answers at once: it asks what to do next, so the instruction goes with the refusal in one step. An empty answer is the old «seguir planeando». Click the terminal and press 3 (or n, or move to the option and ↵); on the phone tap «Seguir planeando».
