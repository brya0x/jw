The composer: grow to a limit, then scroll

Today the composer grows to five rows of text and then shows a window of five rows that follows the cursor (field.rs MAX_TEXT_ROWS, wrap; REQ-254, REQ-256). The window is worked out from the cursor alone on every frame, with no memory, and nothing says that there is more text out of sight. Both terminals below hold the same text and cursor: the left one is what jw does now, the right one is the proposal. Click a terminal and type, or use the buttons.

NOW (5 rows, follows the cursor, no sign)


    
PROPOSAL

  

  

  
Keys: letters type, Enter sends (the text goes to the list), Shift+Enter makes a new line, ← → move, ↑ ↓ move one visible row (REQ-680; on the first or last row they still scroll the list, REQ-682, not drawn here), Home End go to the start or end of the line (as far as the \n, REQ-683). The wheel is drawn over the field only when it is on. The phone has its own composer in Compose and is not part of this.


  
WHAT CHANGES

    
    
      
  • The limit stops being the constant 5 and follows the height of the pane, so a tall terminal gives the person more of what they write, and the list never goes below 4 rows.
    • 
      
  • The window has a place. Now, moving down from the top of a long text keeps the cursor glued to the first row while the text slides under it, because start = min(rows − 5, cursor row) is worked out again every frame. With a place of its own (an offset), the cursor moves inside the window and the text slides only when the cursor reaches an edge, one row at a time.
    • 
      
  • The field says there is more: a thin bar at the right of the text rows with a thumb, and at the right of the chips row ↑3 ↓6 (rows out of sight above and below). Nothing is drawn when everything fits.
    • 
      
  • The wheel over the text rows moves the window without moving the cursor; the next key brings the window back to the cursor.
    • 
      
  • The offset lives beside the text (the Editor or the Ui that holds it), because Field is made again from input on every frame and cannot remember it. REQ-254 stays; REQ-256 changes from «five rows» to «the limit» and gains the window, the bar and the wheel.
    • 
    

  


  
TO DECIDE

    
    
      
  1. The limit. Recommended: a third of the pane's height, never below 5 (24 rows → 8, 36 rows → 12), and never so much that the list keeps fewer than 4 rows. «As now» keeps 5; «what leaves the list 4 rows» lets a long text take almost the whole pane.
    2. 
      
  2. A window that keeps its place (recommended) or the window as it is. It needs one number held beside the text.
    3. 
      
  3. The bar and the count (recommended), or nothing. The bar sits in the columns the field already keeps free at its right (MARKS), so the wrap width does not change.
    4. 
      
  4. The wheel over the field (recommended if the terminal client already receives wheel events over the band: the spec has to check it, REQ-682 only names ↑ and ↓). Off leaves the wheel with the list.
    5. 
      
  5. The name. The type that holds the text is Editor (tui/conversation.rs) and the file that draws it is field.rs, which also draws the shell pane's field. Recommended: rename the type to Composer and leave field.rs as the drawing of any field, in a commit of its own that changes no behaviour. Renaming the file too would put the shell pane's field in a file called composer.
    6. 
      
  6. The phone is left as it is: its composer is Compose and scrolls by itself.
