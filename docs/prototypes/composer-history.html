The composer remembers and folds

Two things the old jw had because the pane ran Claude Code itself, and the composer of today does not: ↑ brings back what was sent before, and a long paste does not fill the field, it shows as a short label with its beginning. Here the composer is drawn as it would be with both. «As now» in each selector gives today's behaviour (no folding, ↑ only moves the cursor and scrolls the list), so the difference can be tried. Click the terminal and type, or paste for real (⌘V) or with the buttons.

COMPOSER


  

  
Keys: letters type, Enter sends, Shift+Enter a new line, ← → move (a label is one step), Backspace erases a label whole, Tab on a label turns it into its text, ↑ on the first row recalls the previous message and ↓ on the last row the next one (past the newest, the draft you were writing comes back). In the history list: ↑ ↓ choose, type to filter, Enter puts it in the field, Esc closes. Five rows of text show at most; the scroll of the field is the other prototype (composer-scroll.html).


  
WHAT CHANGES

    
    
      
  • A long paste folds. Past the limit, what is pasted goes into the line as a label ([Texto pegado #1 +12 líneas «línea 1: texto qu…»]) that works as one character: the cursor steps over it, Backspace erases it whole, Tab opens it into its text. What is sent is the text itself, with the label replaced; nothing is lost. It is the way [Imagen#n] already goes in the line (conversation.rs, attach), and a paste below the limit goes in as it does now.
    • 
      
  • ↑ remembers. On the first row of the field, ↑ puts the previous message in it (the cursor at its start, so another ↑ goes further back); ↓ on the last row goes forward and, past the newest, brings back the draft that was being written. The chips row says ↑ 2/6 while one is recalled. A message that had folded labels comes back folded.
    • 
      
  • ^R is a list of the last messages with a filter, for when ↑ one by one is too slow.
    • 
      
  • The list of the conversation shows a long message folded to three rows and … +N filas más, so a paste does not push the whole conversation out of sight.
    • 
    

  


  
TO DECIDE

    
    
      
  1. When to fold. Recommended: 5 lines or 300 characters. Lower folds almost every paste of a command; higher keeps long pastes in the field.
    2. 
      
  2. ↑ on the first row recalls (recommended: it is what the person remembers). It changes REQ-682, which makes ↑ on the first row scroll the list; the list would keep the wheel and the page keys (to check that they exist). The alternative is ctrl+p / ctrl+n, which the composer has free and would change nothing that exists; the browser owns ctrl+n and this page cannot show it.
    3. 
      
  3. Where the history comes from. (a) The messages of the conversation that the daemon already keeps (Item::User, at most 300, nothing new to store, but a new conversation starts with an empty history), or (b) a file jw keeps for each repository, the last 100, which lasts across conversations as the shell's history does. Recommended: (b). It writes to disk what is typed, pasted text included: skip entries over 20 KB and those that start with a space.
    4. 
      
  4. The history list (^R) is an extra. The key has to be checked against the composer's keys (only ctrl+p and ctrl+n are known to be free). Say «none» to leave it out.
    5. 
      
  5. The long message in the list folded to three rows (recommended) or whole as now. The spec has to look at how the list draws a long message today; it was not checked.
    6. 
      
  6. Order. Both touch the Editor and the field, which feat/composer-scroll is changing: build this after that one is in, and keep the version and the REQ ids past main's.

  7. The phone is left as it is.
